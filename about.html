Why this site exists

There’s more out there
than the usual hits.

Beyond the familiar names, there are small games making their own strange little worlds—often far from the spotlight. Dark Raven Reviews follows those trails, bringing inventive independent projects to players who might otherwise never find them.

We’ll visit the indie hits, too, but our compass points toward creative risks and personal visions: games shaped by the people who made them, with something honest to say. That spark is what keeps us searching.

Every review is a field note from the journey: what a game sets out to do, where its ideas take hold, and who might feel at home in its world.
